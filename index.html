Services

Everyday eye care for every age, from first exams to specialty lenses.

  • Comprehensive Eye Exams

    An unhurried look at how your eyes see, focus and feel — with time to ask questions.

  • Contact Lens (Soft & Specialty)

    Fittings, trials and follow-ups, from daily disposables through to specialty designs.

  • Myopia Control / Ortho-K

    Personalised options, including overnight Ortho-K lenses, for growing eyes.

  • Paediatric Eye Exams

    Gentle, playful visits built around little ones and short attention spans.

  • Diabetic Eye Exams

    Regular check-ins on your eye health, with notes shared back to your care team.

  • Laser Eye Surgery Consult

    A straightforward conversation about whether laser correction suits your eyes and lifestyle.
