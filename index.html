Good to know

OHIP
Covered once a year for patients 19 and under and 65 and over. Between 20 and 64, OHIP pays only if you have diabetes or one of a short list of eye diseases — call us and we will confirm your coverage before you come in.
Insurance
We bill insurance companies directly where we can. Ask about direct billing when you book and we will tell you exactly where you stand.
Driver’s tests
Driver’s-test examinations and OACP examinations are done here, by appointment.
